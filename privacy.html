Privacy policy

How Lyan Studio handles information submitted through this website.

Data we collect

The contact form prepares a message in your email application. It does not submit your details to a website backend or save them in the browser. If you send that email, Lyan Studio receives the name, email address, company or project details and workflow context you include.

Why we collect it

We use this information to reply to messages, discuss project requests and manage business relationships.

Legal basis

The processing is based on legitimate interest and/or pre-contractual exchanges initiated by your request.

How long we keep it

Messages and project-related exchanges may be kept for up to 3 years after the last contact, unless a longer retention period is required by law or an active business relationship continues.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

We only use service providers necessary to host the website and receive messages.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, deletion or objection regarding your personal data by writing to:

hello@lyanstudio.com

Cookies

The website code includes no analytics scripts, advertising cookies or browser storage for contact details. Hosting services may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site.

External product and demo links lead to separate applications whose data handling may differ. If site analytics are added later, this policy will be updated.
