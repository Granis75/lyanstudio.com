Retail / Offline Desktop Software

AlCaisse

Functional prototype · Hardware testing pending

System approach

Keep the sale, stock decrement, shift tracking and backups local to the device. Physical peripherals remain to be validated.

Key modules
Checkout · Stock · Cash sessions · Receipts · Local backup

Electron · React · TypeScript · SQLite
