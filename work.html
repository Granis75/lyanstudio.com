01

Logistics / Mission Operations

Kepler Express

Reference implementation · Public frontend

Kepler Express models one connected process: a delivery mission, the costs it generates, and the invoice and payment follow-up.

The operational problem

Transport and logistics coordination becomes difficult when missions, expenses and billing are maintained separately.

How the work is structured

Bring execution and financial follow-up into one sequence, with expenses linked to the missions that generate them.

Core workflows
Clients · Missions · Driver advances · Expenses · Invoices · Payments
Implementation
React · TypeScript · Supabase

Evidence boundary. A reachable frontend and public source are available. Real operational adoption, production authorization and measured impact are not verified.

02

Neighborhood Retail / Grocery Store

AlCaisse

Client project (developer-reported) · Windows x86 build supplied

AlCaisse connects each checkout to a cash session, a receipt and a stock movement, with data stored locally on Windows.

The operational problem

A neighborhood store needs a checkout workflow that continues when the internet is unavailable and keeps stock consistent with each sale.

How the work is structured

Keep the checkout, cash sessions, inventory and local backup on the Windows machine using SQLite. The developer reports testing the app on Windows.

Core workflows
Checkout · Barcode catalogue · Cash sessions · Inventory · Receipts · Backups
Implementation
Electron · React · TypeScript · SQLite

Evidence boundary. A packaged Windows executable was supplied and inspected. The web simulation is separate; client acceptance, fiscal compliance and peripherals are not independently certified.

Microbusiness / Invoicing & Business Tracking

MyBi

Used in the developer's own business · Private source

MyBi brings client work, issued invoices and payments into one place so an independent professional can follow what remains unpaid.

How the work is structured

Connect client, mission, invoice and payment records rather than maintain unrelated spreadsheets and manual follow-ups.

04

Hospitality / Operational Team Coordination

StayOps

Operations-first platform in development · Source verified

StayOps structures daily hotel work around room readiness, team assignments, incidents and shift handovers.

The operational problem

Hotel operations rely on teams coordinating room readiness, cleaning, incidents and shift changes; reservation records alone do not resolve who does what next.

How the work is structured

Make daily operational work actionable: task ownership, inspection, maintenance blocks, transmissions and inventory movements, with stays retained as context.

Core workflows
Operations today · Housekeeping · Maintenance · Handovers · Linen · Supplies
Implementation
Next.js · React · TypeScript · Supabase

Evidence boundary. The operations-first application is implemented in the merged codebase. The older public demonstration emphasizes reservations rather than the current operations-first product. The application is linked below; new-account onboarding and production access are not yet validated.
